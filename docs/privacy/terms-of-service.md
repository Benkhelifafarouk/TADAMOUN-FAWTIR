## 1. The service

TADAMOUN FAWTIR is a solidarity platform that lets a household submit an essential utility bill
(electricity/gas, water, home internet) for an automated eligibility assessment, and lets donors
contribute to the payment of approved bills or to a general solidarity fund.

The platform is **not** a charity association, **not** a bank, **not** a payment institution and **not**
a public authority. It does not guarantee that a request will be approved or funded.

## 2. Eligibility is decided by an automated engine

- Decisions are taken by a documented, versioned rules engine using the evidence provided.
- A decision may be `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `NEEDS_ADDITIONAL_DOCUMENTS`, `VERIFICATION_UNCERTAIN` or
  `TEMPORARILY_BLOCKED`.
- No employee manually approves ordinary applications. Exceptional administrative interventions are
  restricted, justified and recorded.
- A beneficiary may appeal within the period set by the rules. An appeal creates a new decision; the
  original is preserved.

## 3. Beneficiary obligations

- You must be an adult representing the household concerned.
- You must submit bills that belong to you (or to your household) and information that is accurate.
- You must not submit altered documents, another person's bill, or the same bill more than once.
- You must not create multiple accounts to circumvent assistance limits.
- You must upload only the documents requested, and only when they relate to your household.

The platform may suspend an account, refuse a request or report suspected fraud. Suspension is not a
criminal accusation; a correction and appeal process exists.

## 4. Donor terms

- Donations are voluntary and irrevocable once allocated to a request, except where a refund is expressly
  permitted (unallocated donations).
- You receive an anonymised view; you will never receive the beneficiary's identity, address, account
  number or original bill.
- In the current version, all payments are **simulated** and clearly marked `DEMO`. No money is
  collected and no receipt has accounting value.
- Real payment collection will only be enabled after the operator obtains the required authorisations and
  publishes the corresponding terms.

## 5. Solidarity fund

Contributions to the fund are pooled and allocated to eligible requests according to a published,
auditable policy. Every movement is recorded in an append-only ledger. The fund balance and allocation
history can be reviewed on the transparency page.

## 6. Prohibited conduct

- Attempting to access another user's data or documents.
- Uploading malware, illegal content, or disproportionate personal data.
- Scraping or automating requests in a way that degrades the service.
- Using the platform for money laundering, terrorism financing or any unlawful purpose.
- Impersonating the platform or its staff.

## 7. Availability and changes

The platform is provided “as is” during the pilot phase. Features, limits and rules may change; material
changes to eligibility rules create a new rule version and do not retroactively modify past decisions.
The platform may be interrupted for maintenance or security reasons.
